## THE LAUNCH SEQUENCE (2 WEEKS)

### WEEK 1 — Build & Validate (Days 1–7)

**Days 1–3: Build the tool**
Follow the Relevance AI blueprint. Get a working version of the tool live. Don't perfect it — get it functional. You can refine after you have paying users.

**Days 4–5: Beta test with 5 people**
DM 5 coaches or consultants in your network (not close friends — actual people in your target market). Offer them free access for 48 hours in exchange for feedback. Ask them specifically: Did the content sound like it was written for you? Would you pay for this? What would make it better?

**Days 6–7: Refine based on feedback**
Make adjustments to the system prompt based on what testers said. You're looking for: does the output feel personalized or generic? If generic, the system prompt needs more specificity.

---

### WEEK 2 — Launch (Days 8–14)

**Day 8: Set up Gumroad**
Create your product page. Pricing: $49/month subscription. Use the sales copy from the section below.

**Days 9–11: Content launch sequence**
Post 3 pieces of content across your platforms (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn) that demonstrate the problem the tool solves. These are NOT "buy my tool" posts. They are conversion-psychology posts that make coaches feel the pain of content that doesn't convert. The tool is the natural next step.

**Day 12: Launch post**
One post across all platforms announcing the tool is live. Direct people to the link in bio / Gumroad link.

**Days 13–14: DM outreach**
Personally DM 20 coaches you're connected with. Not a cold pitch — a warm message referencing something specific about their content, then introducing the tool. 20 DMs at your level should yield 3–7 paying users minimum.

## CONTENT TO MARKET THE TOOL (Post these in launch week)

**Post 1 — The Problem (Instagram/TikTok)**
Hook: "Your content isn't the problem. Your strategy is."
Angle: Most coaches post educational content. Educational content builds trust slowly. Conversion content builds trust AND creates urgency. Explain the difference. Don't mention the tool yet.

**Post 2 — The Proof (Instagram/LinkedIn)**
Hook: "I used to post every day and hear nothing. Here's what changed."
Angle: Share a before/after of your own content. One post that performed badly (and why). One that performed well (and the specific thing that made it different). Introduce the tool at the end as "the system I now use."

**Post 3 — The Demo (TikTok/Reel)**
Hook: "Watch me turn a coaching offer into a full week of content in 3 minutes."
Angle: Screen record yourself using the tool. Show the inputs. Show the output. Let the tool sell itself.

**Post 4 — The Launch (All platforms)**
Hook: "The tool is live. Here's how to get access."
Angle: Direct, clear announcement. What it is, who it's for, how to get it. One link. One CTA.
