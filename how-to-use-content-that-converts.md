## How to Get the Best Results

This tool works best when you give it specific details — the more real and specific your inputs, the more powerful your output.

When you open the tool, describe the following in your message:

1. **Your offer** — What do you sell? Be specific. Not "coaching" but "8-week 1:1 program for burnt-out corporate women who want to leave their 9-5."

2. **Your client's pain point** — What keeps them up at night? The more specific, the better the content.

3. **The transformation** — What does their life look like after working with you? Give a real, vivid picture.

4. **The platform** — Tell it where you're posting: Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook, or Email.

5. **Your goal** — Are you trying to attract new followers, nurture your existing audience, or drive direct bookings?

6. **Your tone** — Raw & personal / Professional & authoritative / Warm & conversational / Bold & direct

## Pro Tips

- **Test different tones** for the same offer — bold and direct often outperforms warm on TikTok, while warm and conversational wins on Instagram.
- **Ask for variations** — after your first output, type "give me 3 different hooks for this" to get options to A/B test.
- **Use it weekly** — the more consistently you post conversion content, the faster your audience starts taking action.
